About

I trained as an aerospace engineer: a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering from Boston University (2024) and an M.S. in Space Engineering from the University of Michigan (2026). I now work on AI and automation, designing systems that keep sensitive data on hardware their owners control.

Outside work I build tools I wish existed, which is how Theioma, Tez and the work hub started, and I still like making physical things: the machines on this site began as coursework and keep getting better.

I speak English, Portuguese and Spanish fluently.
