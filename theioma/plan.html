Product · Plan

An interview becomes a plan. Each task has one holder.

The orchestrator turns what you asked for into a plan and tasks. Each task is leased to one session at a time, with a fencing token that only goes up, so two agents never both believe they own it.

Plan

From what you want to who is doing it.

Give the orchestrator a goal for a codebase. This is the plan’s part of the mission, row by row, each with the file that does it.

  1. spoken

    You say what you want, by voice or by typing. The master orchestrator interviews you until the goal is specific, and writes it down. It runs for software and for hardware, and a hardware interview is handed to the engineering project manager.

    master-orchestrator.md

  2. planned

    A project manager owns the plan: phases and tasks in order, each task with the criteria that will say it is done.

    project-manager.md

  3. leased

    Each task is leased to one session at a time. Its writes carry the lease’s fencing token, and a lower token is refused as stale.

    hive/claims.ts

  4. staffed

    Leads spawn workers, each in its own terminal with its own lease. A worker spawns nothing.

    spawn-authority.ts

  5. waits

    A migration, a deploy, anything an agent flags waits in the approvals queue with its exact command.

    approvals-register.tsx

Plan

Every task says where it is.

A blocked task carries its reason. A task nobody holds says so, rather than showing a stale holder; a late write from a holder that lost the task is refused.

Tickets, with their leases.

Every task shows its status, its holder and its fencing token.

  • PENDING planned, not started.
  • IN_PROGRESS a session holds it and is working.
  • BLOCKED waiting on something, with the reason written on the task.
  • IN_REVIEW done by its holder, waiting on the sentinels.
  • APPROVED review passed; COMPLETED done; CANCELLED stopped on purpose.

Plan

One holder, and a token that only goes up.

A work lease names its holder, the holder’s session and computer, and a fencing token. When a lease moves, the token goes up, and a write that still carries the old one is refused as stale.

Plan

A ticket is its whole trace.

Open any task and read what already exists about it. An absence is named, never filled in.

Everything that touched one task.

A ticket is a task joined to its lease. Its page reads the records the work left behind.

  • The claim: holder, computer, session, fencing token, and whether the lease is live.
  • The sessions that worked it, from the lease, the decisions and the usage ledger.
  • The decisions, with their HMAC chain verified.
  • Approvals, artifacts and cost, from the task’s own records.
