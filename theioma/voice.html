Product · Voice

You say it once.

Speak to the sphere or type to it. Speech becomes text, the router picks a model for the turn, the answer comes back as speech, and the master orchestrator takes it from there: it interviews you until the goal is specific, then hands it to a project manager.

Voice

The voice loop, part by part.

Hearing you and speaking back run on your own computer; the model that answers is a local one on Ollama or Claude, as the router picks. A part that is down is named as down in the voice widget, and typing always reaches Theioma.

PartWhat it does
Silero VADHears when you start and stop speaking.
faster-whisperTurns your hands-free speech into text on your own computer.
ParakeetTurns a recording from your phone, or a push-to-talk recording, into text.
The silence gapDecides when your turn is over: a pause ends it.
The routerPicks the model for the turn: a local model on Ollama for routine turns, Claude for the rest.
KokoroSpeaks the answer as it streams, clause by clause.
The sphereShows the state: listening, thinking, speaking or muted, each named in words beside it.

Voice

What you said becomes a goal.

The master orchestrator is an agent definition with its own brief and tools. On a new project it interviews you; on a returning one it presents the brief and carries on.

The interview
It asks until the goal is specific enough to plan, and writes it down. It runs for software and for hardware.
The hand-off
It staffs the goal with a project manager and never writes code itself.
A machine
A hardware project is handed to the engineering project manager, which runs the design method stage by stage.
On your desktop
Desktop control is built for Windows, behind approval; its driver starts only when you name and arm it.
