Product · Sentinels

Sentinels check every task. Then you decide.

A worker’s code is reviewed before its task closes: a code sentinel, then a security sentinel, each reports pass or fail, and a fail sends the work back with its feedback. Whatever is consequential after that, a migration, a print, a flash, a click on your desktop, waits for you.

The review chainThen your approval

Sentinels

The review chain, as the manager runs it.

Written in the project manager’s own definition. The reviewers are agents too, spawned into their own terminals like the worker they review.

StepWhoWhat it checksThen
1code-sentinelTypeScript strict, dead code, the repository’s patternspass, or fail with its findings
2security-sentinelOWASP, secrets, authpass, or fail with its findings
3the project managerboth passedmarks the task complete; a fail re-spawns the worker with the feedback, three times at most

From project-manager.md, “Review chain (code tasks only)”. Design and HTML tasks skip it. A sentinel reviews on whichever computer holds the task’s lease.

What the reviewers have raised, on record.

Every verdict is a record. From 23 Jul 2026 to 24 Aug 2026, the code, security and QA reviewers filed these; the Benchmark Room says what they can and cannot be read as.

  • 84review records, each one reviewer’s verdict on one task
  • 62findings raised (every record that is not a clean pass)
  • 22clean passes
  • 5tasks where a finding was followed by a pass from the same reviewer

A record, not a result: these say what was found, not that the reviews made the work better. The full entry, with its caveats.
