What Theioma does, area by area.

This page says, area by area, what Theioma does, and where the evidence is written down.

The receipts across computers

Status

By area.

Each area below is a capability of the product, in the words of the README’s Status table. 06-GAP-SUMMARY.md holds the file-and-line evidence.

AreaWhat it does
Many computersEach computer you own joins with its own device key under your account’s certificate authority, over mutual TLS 1.3. Work is leased to the computer that fits it, a lease is fenced, and a revoked computer is cut off at its next call. The receipts are three node processes on one computer; two physical computers come after v1.0.
AgentsClaude Code, Codex and Cursor have each run as an agent under a steward, in a real terminal; OpenClaw and any HTTP agent have passed a probe; opencode, Gemini CLI and Aider are rows not verified yet.
ModelsOne catalogue of models, a router that picks a tier for each task, and a usage ledger for model use.
Phone approvalsPair a phone with a QR code and answer approvals from it: one tap at low risk, your passkey for anything consequential. An approval answered on a physical phone is not yet proven.
Voice and desktopSpeak to Theioma and it speaks back; the best measured spoken turn took 8.2 s, and no path is under one second yet. Desktop control is built for Windows, behind your approval, and its driver starts only when you name and arm it.
LearningFeedback and outcomes are captured, and lessons are filed with their source. The nightly passes run and record each step; none has yet proposed and applied an edit live. The monthly fine-tune is a dry run that records itself and trains nothing, and the one local fine-tune trained so far was refused by its own held-out gate.
J-PEX hardwareA sentence becomes a researched, simulated, editable electromechanical design: requirements, parts, CAD, circuit, firmware, simulation, documents and a print approval, each stage granted only by a check that ran. A drone and a robot arm have been through every core room; no print or firmware flash has been sent by Theioma. The product has now run its own chain on a second drone: an engineering PM started the room stewards and a steward started its workers, though a stage does not yet close cleanly.
PackagingTheioma runs from source on Windows, and the desktop shell builds unsigned Windows installers from the same source. No installer is published, and macOS and Linux come after v1.0.
